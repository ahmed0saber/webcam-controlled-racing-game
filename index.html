How to play

  • Camera: enable the webcam, show both hands like a steering wheel, then calibrate straight ahead.
  • Turning the wheel left steers left. Turning it right steers right.
  • Keyboard: A / Left and D / Right steer. The car cruises. W / Up adds speed, S / Down brakes.
  • Space pauses. Esc opens the pause menu. Collect coins, grab boosts, and avoid traffic.
